## Overview
Heist is a side-content mechanic where you hire Rogues to infiltrate secure facilities. Contracts are single-run heists; Blueprints are multi-wing grand heists with curio displays containing high-value rewards. Heist drops its own currency (Rogue Markers) and exclusive reward types (replicas, alt-quality gems, enchanted armaments).

## How to Run
1. Allocate Atlas passives for Heist (contract/blueprint drops, marker quantity)
2. Level Rogues and equip Rogue Gear for maximum efficiency
3. Run Contracts to generate Rogue Markers and reveal Blueprint wings
4. Grand Heist Blueprints are the main profit — target Currency and Gem reward rooms
5. Sell Rogue Markers, Replicas, and alt-quality gems in bulk
