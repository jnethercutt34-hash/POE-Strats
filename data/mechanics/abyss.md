# Abyss

## Metadata
- **Patch Version:** 3.25
- **Scaling Tags:** ["quantity", "abyss_depth", "map_tier"]
- **Is Scarab Heavy:** true

## Overview
Abyss cracks open in maps, spawning waves of monsters along a moving trail. The crack can lead to an Abyssal Depths — a side area with a boss that can drop Abyss Jewels (Murderous, Searching, Hypnotic, Ghastly) and Stygian Vises. The Lich boss at the end of deeper Depths can drop valuable unique jewels.

## How to Run
1. Allocate Atlas passives for Abyss (extra abysses, Depths chance, jewel quality)
2. Use Abyss Scarabs for guaranteed additional Abysses
3. Follow the crack quickly — it despawns if you fall behind
4. Target Abyssal Depths for boss drops and Stygian Vises
5. Sell Abyss Jewels with good mods (life + crit multi + flat damage)
