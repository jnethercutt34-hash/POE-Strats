# Essence

## Metadata
- **Patch Version:** 3.25
- **Scaling Tags:** ["quantity", "essence_tier", "corruption"]
- **Is Scarab Heavy:** true

## Overview
Essence-trapped monsters appear in maps, frozen by crystals. Clicking the crystals releases them with guaranteed Essence drops. Essences are powerful deterministic crafting currency — each guarantees a specific mod on an item. Higher-tier Essences (Deafening, Shrieking) and corrupt-only Essences (Hysteria, Insanity, Horror, Delirium) are highly valuable.

## How to Run
1. Allocate Atlas passives for Essence (extra essences, tier upgrades, corruption)
2. Use Essence Scarabs for additional Essence monsters per map
3. Use Remnant of Corruption on high-tier monsters for a chance at corrupt-only Essences
4. Sell Deafening Essences in bulk — Deafening Essence of Zeal/Contempt are premium
5. Corrupt Essences with 4 modifiers for a chance at upgrading to corrupt-only types
