# Expedition

## Metadata
- **Patch Version:** 3.25
- **Scaling Tags:** ["quantity", "remnant_mods", "logbook"]
- **Is Scarab Heavy:** true

## Overview
Expedition encounters let you detonate ruins to reveal monsters with modular difficulty/reward via Remnant modifiers. The tradeable outputs are Logbooks (high-value maps), Exotic Coinage, Scrap Metal, Astragali, and Burial Medallions used at NPC vendors (Gwennen, Tujen, Rog, Dannig). Logbooks themselves are the jackpot drops.

## How to Run
1. Allocate Atlas passives for Expedition (remnant and logbook nodes)
2. Use Expedition Scarabs to guarantee encounters
3. Always chain Remnant modifiers that increase quantity/rarity
4. Sell Logbooks in bulk — Dannig and Tujen Logbooks are most valuable
5. Use NPC currencies at vendors: Tujen for raw currency, Gwennen for unique gambling
