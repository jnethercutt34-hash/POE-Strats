# Ritual

## Metadata
- **Patch Version:** 3.25
- **Scaling Tags:** ["quantity", "pack_size", "map_tier"]
- **Is Scarab Heavy:** true

## Overview
Ritual altars spawn in maps, letting you fight waves of monsters for Tribute points. Tribute is spent at a shop of deferred rewards — currency, uniques, and special Ritual bases. Deferring expensive items across maps builds toward big payouts. Atlas passives increase the pool of available items and reroll chances.

## How to Run
1. Allocate Atlas passives for Ritual (extra altars, reroll favours, item quantity)
2. Use Ritual Scarabs for guaranteed encounters with better base rewards
3. Run T16 maps for maximum tribute generation
4. Defer high-value items and reroll for them in subsequent maps
5. Ritual vessels can store encounters for double-dipping tribute
