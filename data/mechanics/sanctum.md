## Overview
The Forbidden Sanctum is a roguelike dungeon where you navigate 4 floors of rooms, managing Resolve (health) and gaining Inspiration (buffs). Rewards scale with depth — Floor 4 has the best loot. Relics and Sanctified Relics are the exclusive high-value drops. The mechanic rewards careful pathing and build optimization for sustained damage without getting hit.

## How to Run
1. Allocate Atlas passives for Sanctum (extra Sanctum encounters, better Relics)
2. Build for high evasion/dodge — Resolve loss from hits is the main limiting factor
3. Prioritize Inspiration boons that reduce Resolve loss or grant damage
4. Target Floor 4 for Sanctified Relics and Divine Font rewards
5. Sell Sanctified Relics and valuable Forbidden Flame/Flesh jewels
