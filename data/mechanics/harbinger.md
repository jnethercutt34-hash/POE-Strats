# Harbinger

## Metadata
- **Patch Version:** 3.25
- **Scaling Tags:** ["quantity", "pack_size", "harbinger_count"]
- **Is Scarab Heavy:** true

## Overview
Harbinger encounters spawn a Harbinger boss that summons waves of blue monsters. They drop Currency Shards that combine into full orbs — including exclusive shards for Harbinger's Orb, Ancient Orb, Orb of Annulment, and Orb of Horizons. Harbingers are efficient for passive currency generation alongside other mechanics.

## How to Run
1. Allocate Atlas passives for Harbinger (extra Harbingers, shard quantity)
2. Use Harbinger Scarabs for additional Harbingers per map
3. Kill the summoned waves quickly — Harbinger becomes vulnerable as adds die
4. Sell currency shards in bulk or combine into full orbs
5. Pair with other speed-farming mechanics for maximum maps/hour
