# Incursion

## Metadata
- **Patch Version:** 3.25
- **Scaling Tags:** ["quantity", "incursion_tier", "map_tier"]
- **Is Scarab Heavy:** true

## Overview
Incursion sends you back in time to the Temple of Atzoatl, where you upgrade rooms by killing Architects. After 12 incursions, you open the completed Temple. Room tier determines reward quality — Tier 3 rooms like Apex of Ascension, Locus of Corruption, and Doryani's Institute are the jackpots. Corruption rooms can double-corrupt items for massive value.

## How to Run
1. Allocate Atlas passives for Incursion (room upgrades, Alva missions)
2. Use Incursion Scarabs for extra incursions per map
3. Prioritize upgrading high-value rooms: Corruption Chamber, Gem room, Currency room
4. Target T3 Locus of Corruption for double-corrupt gambles
5. Sell completed Temples with valuable room combinations
